My Favorite Music Artist

By: Bella Pope

As you know, my favorite genres are pop, indie, and R&B.

But as for my favorite artist, it's definitely SZA. Sza is an american woman who is a singer and songwriter who blends r&B, hiphop, soul and other genres. Last May I was able to see her at Xcel Energy Center in Minneapolis where she performed with Kendrick Lamar.
