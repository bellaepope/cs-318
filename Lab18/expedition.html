The 2026 Deep Survey Mission

Our research vessel departs this autumn for a 60-day expedition into the Hadal Zone — the deepest parts of the ocean, found only in oceanic trenches. Using a next-generation remotely operated vehicle (ROV), we will descend to depths never before explored by our team.

Equipped with 4K low-light cameras, sediment samplers, and specimen collection arms, the ROV will transmit live footage from up to 10,000 meters below sea level. Our goals include cataloguing new species, collecting water samples for climate research, and mapping previously uncharted seafloor terrain.

Mission Objectives

  1. Map 2,000 km² of unexplored seafloor terrain
  2. Collect sediment core samples from the Hadal Zone
  3. Catalog and document new species candidates
  4. Measure microplastic contamination at extreme depths
  5. Test new bioluminescence detection sensors

Mission Stats

60 Days at sea
10km Maximum dive depth
12 Crew & scientists aboard
3 ROVs deployed
